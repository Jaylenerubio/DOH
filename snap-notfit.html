Other food support

Are you pregnant, just had a baby, or have kids under five? Get extra food help through WIC⁠.

If you’re 60 or older, you may qualify for a free monthly food package and farmers market vouchers through the Commodity Supplemental Food Program⁠. You can get this and SNAP at the same time.
