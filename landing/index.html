You’re eligible for the Enhanced Energy Affordability Program (EEAP) What’s next

1

Start your EEAP application

If your electric and natural gas services are with different companies, you can apply for both in a single application — as long as both accounts are at the same address.

2

You have 14 days after applying to submit your proof of income for the last 28 days for everyone you share your utility costs with.

That includes paystubs, Social Security, unemployment and disability payments, and child support. Photos and uploaded documents are both accepted.

3

Once approved, you’ll receive the EAP discount within two billing cycles.

Your credit will last 18 months. You’ll need to reapply after that.

Frequently asked questions

The Health and Affordability Resource Team (HART) Connector is a digital tool that helps New Yorkers lower their cost of living. It finds services you may qualify for and connects you to them, including:

  • Health insurance enrollment
  • Food assistance
  • Help to prevent eviction
  • Utility discounts
  • Financial help with hospital bills
  • Cash back from the IRS

The NYC Department of Health and Mental Hygiene (NYC Health) works with other city agencies and partner organizations to make enrollment easier. Our partners will help you from the start of your application until you get the benefit or service.

HART community health workers use the tool to check which programs you may qualify for and to follow up with you. The tool also sends messages and reminders to help you finish each application.

The HART Connector helps New Yorkers who may be struggling to afford basic needs pay for healthcare, utilities, groceries, and more — at no cost.

To make it easier to sign up for more than one service, NYC Health will collect and store your:

  • Full name
  • Date of birth
  • ZIP code
  • Contact information

NYC Health will also ask about your income, your household, and any bills you need help with, like utility or hospital bills. This information is used to find partner services you may qualify for.

NYC Health tracks your progress on each application and sends you reminders by email, phone, or text. You can opt out of these messages at any time.

If you choose to use a partner’s services, NYC Health shares the information that partner needs to help you. Some partners may ask you to agree to their own privacy policy and terms of use first.

NYC Health may use data from the HART Connector and its partners to study and improve the program. Your data is kept secure. Anything NYC Health shares publicly won’t include information that identifies you.
