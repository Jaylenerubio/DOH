What you should
know before we begin

The NYC Health and Affordability Navigator connects you to services you qualify for to lower the cost of living.

To simplify the process of enrolling in multiple services, the NYC Department of Health and Mental Hygiene (“NYC Health”) will collect and save your full name, date of birth, zip code, and contact information.

Our partners will help you get to the finish line. If you pick an NYC Health partner organization’s services, we will also share your information with them so you don’t need to re-enter it. Your activity with our partners will remain linked to your account on this platform.

How we use your information

  1. To best serve you, we will track your enrollment progress and contact you with reminders by email, phone, or text. You may opt out at any time.
  2. If you choose to use a partner organization’s services, we will share the information they need to help you. Some partners may ask you to read and agree to their own privacy rules before they assist you.
  3. NYC Health may use data from this platform and our partners to study, evaluate, and improve the program. All of your data will be handled in a secure manner. Anything we share publicly will not include identifying information.

Learn more about NYC Health’s privacy policy.
