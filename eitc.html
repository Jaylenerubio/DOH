Let’s see if you qualify for a cash refund from the IRS.

Tax credits like the Earned Income Tax Credit give working people cash back. You may qualify even if you don’t earn much, don’t usually have to file taxes, or have never filed before. Your chances are even better if you take care of children.

Missed out in the past three years? You can still file or amend those returns to get that money back.

Answer a few quick questions to find out if you can claim tax credits.

Have you filed taxes in the last three years?
Did you have income from work in the last three years?
Do you have children that you care for?
